# Nokia welcome asset

`nokia_welcome.mp3` is the same Nokia Tune 8800 rendition selected for the
console's welcome screen, copied from the locally downloaded `Music/Nokia Tune.mp3`.
Source: https://www.nokioteca.net/blog/2008/05/nokia-tune-optimized/

The recording is embedded as compressed MP3 data in flash, not a RAM array.
It is decoded to the MAX98357A over I2S. Attribution/source information does
not grant redistribution rights to the recording.
